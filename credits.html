Credits

Original game artwork and renders by Rare Limited and Nintendo.

Section titles are set in Limelight by Sorkin Type, used under the SIL Open Font License.

The look follows Rare’s own Rarewhere site as it stood in 2000. CyStation started out on Sadgrl’s layout builder (opens in a new tab).

The ads and trailers on the Rare TV and the iQue TV belong to Nintendo, Rare, iQue and Keebler. They’re trimmed and shrunk from copies saved by GamesDatabase.org (opens in a new tab), the Internet Archive (opens in a new tab), the Retro Games TV and iQue Research Group (opens in a new tab) YouTube channels, and other collectors.

The iQue Player and iQue Card photos are by Evan-Amos (opens in a new tab), who released them into the public domain on Wikimedia Commons. The page itself borrows the look of iQue’s own website from 2004, as kept by the Wayback Machine (opens in a new tab).

Patch credits live with each project: the Perfect Dark and Tomorrow Never Dies 64 pages list the decompilation, mod and music credits.
