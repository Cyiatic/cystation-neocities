Credits

Original game artwork and renders by Rare Limited and Nintendo.

The timeline month lettering uses the Banjo-Kazooie and Banjo-Tooie dialogue font, ripped by Malek Masoud.

Section titles are set in Limelight by Sorkin Type, used under the SIL Open Font License.

The look follows Rare’s own Rarewhere site as it stood in 2000. CyStation started out on Sadgrl’s layout builder (opens in a new tab).

Patch credits live with each project: the Perfect Dark and Tomorrow Never Dies 64 pages list the decompilation, mod and music credits.
