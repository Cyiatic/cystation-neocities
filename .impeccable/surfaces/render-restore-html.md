# Render Restore

- **Mode:** Experience.
- **Audience and job:** Preservation-minded visitors and retro-game players who want to inspect recovered render sets, understand what changed, and reach the source files without wading through a general blog.
- **Action:** Open the newest filing, scan its numbered set register, then follow the external source or a dedicated collection entry.
- **Proof and content:** The Conker filing opens in a manually pannable viewer using seven local copies of the original panorama strips. An expandable research section preserves dimensions, dates, ROM cross-reference and original external links. The N64 New Soft Catalog mark remains a related filed item.
- **Direction:** A broadcast teletext magazine adapted to the established CyStation station desk. The index leads with a numbered RR channel instrument and latest filing, then keeps older entries in a compact dated register.
- **Memorable moment:** The visitor arrives inside the Conker panorama and can move between seven locations; the archive record is one click away.
- **Constraints:** Keep the deep-blue/gold/cream/pale-blue visual world, preserve source links and research, use manual motion with keyboard alternatives, and keep every entry usable on narrow screens. The viewer presents strips, not reconstructed 3D geometry.
- **Unresolved decisions:** Attach the public Internet Archive item URL and per-file URLs when the first external set is filed.
