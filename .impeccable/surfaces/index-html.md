# CyStation homepage

- **Mode:** Experience.
- **Visitor job:** reach a released patch (its menu or its downloads) or one of the three areas within one click.
- **Composition:** bar; centred CYSTATION wordmark; Conker-on-the-R render beside a one-line intro and "Out now" (PD, TND) with gold buttons and download links; then Patches row (Diddy's plane), Render Restore plate (Windy panorama), Timeline row (Scribes quill, flipped); Outposts footer.
- **Constraints:** two released patches only; in-development projects named without downloads; no carousel, card grid, eyebrow labels or route-card blocks; about two desktop viewports.

## Direction contract

THESIS: CyStation is a Rarewhere-style station: a navy column where engraved gold titles and real renders announce each destination. It refuses the category default of a hero banner, card grid and footer link farm.
OWN-WORLD: flat #000060, cream Georgia copy, #FFDD00 links, Limelight gilt with a 3px hatch, the Arial Black offset-shadow wordmark, an italic gold bar with gold end caps, gold bevel buttons, googly-eyed Outposts icons.
STORY: visitors meet CyStation, see the two released patches with their actions, then pick Patches, Render Restore or Timeline from character-led rows.
FIRST VIEWPORT: bar at top; wordmark centred about 70px tall; render (17.5rem) left of the intro, Out now, and both releases with "Enter Perfect Dark" and "Open the dossier" buttons, all above the fold at 1280×800 and 390×844.
FORM: Rarewhere 2000 hub page (Recent Rare / Retro Rare grammar), pinned by the owner; seed: none (direction pinned by the owner, roll not run).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
