Privacy

CyStation is a static fan site. It has no accounts, sign-ups, forms, comments, cookies or ads, and it doesn’t ask you for anything.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Neocities (opens in a new tab). Like any web host, Neocities receives your IP address and browser details when it serves a page, and it keeps basic view counts for the site owner. Its handling of that data is covered by the Neocities privacy policy (opens in a new tab).

Analytics

CyStation runs no analytics or tracking scripts of its own. The only visit numbers are the host’s view counts described above.

Settings kept in your browser

The Tomorrow Never Dies 64 and Perfect Dark menus remember your sound and motion choices in your browser’s local storage. Those settings stay on your device and are never sent anywhere; clearing your site data removes them.

Other sites

Downloads and source files open on GitHub, uploads on the Internet Archive, and some full-size originals on Imgur. Videos and updates live on YouTube and X (Twitter). Those services only see you when you follow a link to them, and their own privacy policies apply there.
